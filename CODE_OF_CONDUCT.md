# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Showing empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility, apologising to those affected by our mistakes, and
  learning from the experience
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without their explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Project-specific note

This project builds software that records people. Discussions about consent,
privacy and the ethics of recording are welcome and expected.

Advocating for using OpenMurmur to record people covertly, or asking for help
doing so, is not. It will be treated as a Code of Conduct violation, not as a
technical question.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the project maintainer through GitHub. All
complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The privacy
and security of the reporter will be respected.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may
face temporary or permanent repercussions.
